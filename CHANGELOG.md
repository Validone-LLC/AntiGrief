# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-26)

First public release, for WoW: Forever 1.60.1.

- Flags Need rolls on items that don't suit the roller: armor type, weapon skills (including Forever's rogue axes and druid polearms), level requirement, stats and "Equip:" effects, and an upgrade check by item level or Pawn scale.
- Severity levels Low, Medium and High, with an adjustable alert threshold.
- Follows flagged Need wins, and checks about 2 minutes later whether the winner has equipped the item.
- Two sounds: the raid-warning horn when a flagged player wins the item, and an alarm when they still haven't equipped it. Need rolls are silent.
- Private chat alerts and an on-screen banner; optional, rate-limited group announcements.
- Fit report on new drops, a badge under the loot roll window, and item tooltip lines.
- Saved log with a log window (filters, per-player totals) and an "AG Logs" chat tab.
- Repeat counter per dungeon run, an ignore list, and friends and guildmates skipped by default.
- Options panel, minimap button with its own menu, AddOns-menu entry and `/ag` commands.
